# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, constructive, and assume good faith.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect maintainers' time — search existing issues before opening new ones.
- No harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information, or
  sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
violate this code, and may ban temporarily or permanently anyone who
engages in unacceptable behavior. Report concerns privately to the
repository maintainers. All complaints are reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.
