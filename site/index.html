why it exists

fewer tokens, honestly

A tight system prompt, lazy tool definitions, and output shaping mean every turn costs less. The ledger shows the receipts — tokens, cache hits, latency, and dollars per task.

local-first

Sessions, checkpoints, skills, and the usage ledger live on your machine. No account wall between you and your repo - sign in only for the provider you choose.

provider-agnostic

OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic adapters, Nous Portal OAuth, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a model on localhost. Switch mid-chat; the app keeps up.

mcp native

Connect Blender, Godot, or any MCP server from Settings and its tools join the same transcript, permissions, and ledger as built-in ones.

29 palettes + glass

From phosphor green to sakura night, every palette ships with the app - plus an experimental frosted-glass mode.

checkpoints & rewind

Every turn is journaled. Rewind a bad edit, regenerate a reply, or compact a bloated context without losing the thread.
